# KIAAN HRM - COMPANY POLICY HANDBOOK (KNOWLEDGE BASE)

## 1. Attendance Policy
* Working Hours: Standard check-in is between 09:00 AM and 09:30 AM.
* Late Check-in: Checking in after 09:30 AM is marked as "Late". A grace period of 15 minutes may apply based on company settings. Repeated lateness (more than 3 times in a month) might trigger automatic salary deductions.
* Absenteeism: Not checking in by 12:00 PM without prior leave approval marks the day as "Absent".

## 2. Leave Policy
* Annual Leave: Every employee is entitled to 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
* Sick Leave: 10 days of paid sick leave per year (medical certificate required for >2 days).
* Emergency Leave: 5 days per year for sudden emergencies.
* Unpaid Leave: Up to 20 days per year, subject to management approval.
* Submission: All leaves must be submitted at least 24 hours in advance via the dashboard or platform assistant.

## 3. Expense Claims & Reimbursements
* Eligible Claims: Travel, client entertainment, office supplies, and training/certifications.
* Submission Limit: Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date along with receipts.
* Approval: Claims are approved by the company HR or Admin. Paid reimbursements are processed with the monthly payroll cycle.

## 4. Shift & Work Location
* Geofencing: Employees must punch in/out from within the company's designated geofence radius. Punching outside the geofence will fail or register as an alert.
* Shift Changes: Shift schedules are assigned by the manager. Any shift swaps require 48 hours notice.
